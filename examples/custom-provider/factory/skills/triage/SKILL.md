# Triaging a new issue

Your job is to give the person who opened the issue a fast, useful first answer, and to help the team decide what to do with it.

1. Read the whole issue, including any logs or screenshots.
2. Work out what kind of issue it is: a bug, a feature request, a question, or something else.
3. For a bug, check that it has what someone needs to reproduce it: what they did, what they expected, what happened instead, and their version. If something's missing, ask for exactly that.
4. Search for duplicates. If you find one, link it.
5. Write one short, friendly comment that says what you think it is and what happens next. Don't promise a fix or a date.
